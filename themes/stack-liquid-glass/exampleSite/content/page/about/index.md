This is the **Liquid Glass** theme demo. The whole site is rendered from `themes/stack-liquid-glass/exampleSite/`.

If you're evaluating the theme for your own blog, every page you can see here is reproducible from the four files in `exampleSite/content/post/`.

## Why glass?

Most blog themes flatten everything into solid panels. Glass gives you depth without losing legibility — provided you keep contrast tight and the moving parts slow. This theme errs on the side of calm.
